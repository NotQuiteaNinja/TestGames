Track 8 is described by the source as a Spy vs. Spy arrangement with some fixed patterns. Track 17 is The Stationary Ark (Low pH Arktic Clubmix), also with some fixed parts. Titles are preserved rather than replaced with invented song names.

The existing six BPM values in `bpm.json` are estimates from the original game, not official tempo metadata. They remain unchanged. New tracks have no BPM pin; the web player uses live audio analysis for its reactive visuals. The manifest records measured durations and SHA-256 digests for all recordings.
